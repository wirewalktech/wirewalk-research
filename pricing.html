Purchasing & project terms

A structure your team
can budget for.

For larger projects, the usual structure is 40% at acceptance, 40% at a demonstrated milestone, and 20% at handover. The signed proposal sets the actual milestones and payment terms.

Read engagement terms →

Universities, labs, and procurement teams

Request a written quotation, vendor information, and a purchase order or invoice arrangement. Invoice terms are agreed in writing; submitting an inquiry does not approve credit or start work.

Card and bank transfer

Directly bookable services use Stripe Checkout. Card and ACH have the same published fee, with no automatic prepayment or bank discount. Bank payments may take several business days to clear.

Optional costs stay visible

We separate engineering fees from cloud/GPU rental, API usage, hardware, storage, licensing, and travel. Client-owned subscriptions and approved spending caps keep running costs visible.
